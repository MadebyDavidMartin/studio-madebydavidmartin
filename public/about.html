Leadership

Creative leadership.

Creative leadership is not about having the loudest idea in the room. It is about creating the conditions for good ideas to survive.

  1. 01Give people a clear brief.
  2. 02Create room for experimentation.
  3. 03Know when to challenge the work.
  4. 04Know when to get out of the way.
  5. 05Protect the quality bar.
  6. 06Connect creative decisions to business objectives.
  7. 07Translate between creative, technical and business teams.
  8. 08Make the decision when the room cannot agree.

The best creative teams do not need someone telling them what to do every minute. They need someone who can give them direction worth following.

Craft

Direction vs. execution.

I am comfortable getting hands-on. I write. I storyboard. I generate. I edit. I build. I experiment. I learn new tools. But hands-on production is not the same thing as production for its own sake. The reason I stay close to the work is so I understand what is actually possible. That lets me direct with more specificity.

From “we should make something cinematic” to “let’s separate the character identity pass from the camera motion, build the environment independently, and assemble the final sequence in post.”

The closer I get to the technology, the better I can direct the creative. I don’t need to do everything myself. I need to understand enough to know what is possible.
