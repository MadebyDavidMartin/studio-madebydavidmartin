Production steps

The whole job in order. Ticks save in this browser only. Each reference and shot card below has its own Done box too; the counter on the left covers everything.

Concept

Quiet luxury: not the fastest EV, the calmest. The sky warms steadily from shot 1 to shot 14 and the soundscape grows with it. That progression is the spine of the edit.

Beats 1 and 2 · 0 to 16 s

Blue hour, departure

Near-black. The light bar wakes from its centre outward. The car sits by a cedar house in mist. Her hands rest on the wool wheel. The car rolls out over wet gravel with no sound but the gravel.

Beats 3 and 4 · 16 to 32 s

The road, the details

From above, the car is a single point of light on a cliff road over a misty inlet. Music enters on one piano note. Fir reflections slide over graphite paint, an aero wheel turns, rain beads on the glass roof, a raven calls.

Beats 5 and 6 · 32 to 48 s

Her eyes, first light

Her calm eyes in the mirror. The window drops a few centimetres and the world gets louder. The sun clears the ridge; gold light rakes along the car and the bronze flake ignites. The music swells.

Beats 7 and 8 · 48 to 60 s

The lookout, end card

The music drops away. She stands by the parked car with a cup, back to us, facing the sunrise. Fade to black. The chrome emblem, SOLENTEI, "Move beyond the noise." One bird, then silence.

Brand and car

SOLENTEI 369 (so-LEN-tay, three six nine) is a fictional ultra-premium electric marque. Philosophy: harmony, energy, completeness. Renamed from Eastmere on 19 Oct 2026; file names keep the AUR_ prefix and nothing generated needs to change, because no shot shows a badge.

The car, rule by rule

PaintLiquid graphite with warm bronze flake. Never black, silver or blue.
LightsOne ultra-thin bar each end. Warm white front, deep amber rear.
WheelsClosed aero discs, satin graphite, small bronze centre dot.
BadgesNone on the car. The emblem lives only in the graphics.
InteriorStone-grey wool, pale ash wood, one slim floating screen, almost no buttons.
PlatesNone. If one appears, edit the still to remove it.

Identity

  • Emblem: David's three woven silver bands, used exactly as supplied, in polished chrome. Never redrawn or recoloured.
  • Wordmark: SOLENTEI in Marcellus capitals, tracked wide, chrome finish. 369 sits beside or beneath it in Jost ExtraLight, small and quiet.
  • Tagline: WHERE ENERGY FINDS HARMONY. in Jost Light capitals, tracked wide, silver.
  • Voice: few words, unhurried, no specs, no exclamation marks, no superlatives.
  • Never more than five words on screen at once.

Look and sky

Natural light, slow camera, deep shadow. It should feel shot on a large-format cinema camera at dawn, not rendered. The sky is locked to five stages; each shot card shows its stage and the exact phrase is already inside its prompt.

SKY AS01 to S04 · deep blue pre-dawn, dark navy, no sun
SKY BS05 to S09 · late blue hour, faint pale band in the east
SKY CS10, S11 · eastern horizon turning soft rose
SKY DS12, S13 · sun's edge clears the ridge, rose-gold
SKY ES14 · just after sunrise, gold on the water

Camera

  • Slow and smooth: gimbal at car height, slow aerial drifts, locked-off macros, gentle push-ins.
  • One camera move per clip. No shaky cam, no whip pans.
  • Keep the car and horizon in the middle of frame: the 2.39 letterbox crops top and bottom.

Grade and texture (Claude, in the edit)

  • Cool shadows, the warming progression reinforced shot by shot.
  • Soft halation on highlights and light bars.
  • Uniform 35 mm grain added in the edit, never asked for in prompts.
  • Accent colour is the car's bronze. No saturated reds, greens or neon.

How to prompt

Two models, two jobs. Nano Banana Pro locks the look in a still. Omni Flash 1.1 only has to move it.

Stills

Nano Banana Pro

  • Every reference, start frame, end frame and the key visual.
  • Generate at 2K, 16:9 (9:16 for V1 to V3, 4K for the key visual). A sharper start frame gives Omni more detail to hold.
  • Prompts are full sentences in photographer's language: camera height, lens, light direction, time of day.
  • Attach the references listed on each card. It holds the car best when the car reference and the place reference are both attached.
  • When a still is nearly right, edit it: "Edit this image. Only change: ... Nothing else changes." End frames are always made this way from the approved start frame.
Animation

Omni Flash 1.1, Frames to Video

  • The start frame already defines the look, so motion prompts describe movement only. Re-describing the scene invites Omni to redraw it.
  • One slow camera move and one slow subject move. Omni is strong on light, mist, water, reflections and camera moves; weak on hands, fingers and walking bodies.
  • Every prompt ends with the shape lock, an Audio line and "One continuous, unbroken shot, no cuts." Omni makes sound with the picture and invents music or speech unless told what to play.
  • Render 6 s (8 s for S02, S05, S13, S14). Download at 1080p where Flow offers it.
  • Too slow is fixable in the edit; too fast is not. Keep the speed words.
  • A moving car must stay in its own lane. Let the camera match its speed rather than asking the car to move across the frame, and say the road and lane markings stay exactly as shown.
  • Keep mist far from the car and still. Moving vapour near the car or past the windscreen reads as exhaust or smoke, which an electric car must never show.

After two or three failed attempts on a shot, stop. Go back to the last prompt that gave a usable frame, or use the fallback on the card.

Shots

Fourteen shots for the 60 s hero. For each: make the keyframe in Nano Banana Pro with the listed references attached, approve it, then animate it in Omni Flash 1.1 with the keyframe as the first frame. Where an end frame is listed, make it by editing the approved keyframe and set it as the last frame. Make keyframes in story order so the sky stays honest. Music and sound

Sound design carries the idea, so it gets as much care as the picture. Generate the score on Suno Pro so commercial rights are covered. Nature sounds come from real recordings: synthetic ones read as static on OCCUPIED.

How the score sits (v2)

  • Music runs under the whole film, carrying it. Solo piano from 0:01, strings from about 0:12.
  • Steady build through the road and detail shots, always under the voice.
  • Climax at sunrise, 0:40 to 0:48, the loudest the music gets.
  • Settles over the lookout and resolves on one long chord under the end card.
  • Sound effects are a quiet bed underneath, about 12 dB below the music, never louder than the voice.
  • Mix about -16 LUFS, true peak -1.5 dB.
