Flag on the Play: Production Guide

How a 23.5-second big-game spec commercial was made: a packed stadium stuck on hold, a flamingo referee half the length of a football, and a full broadcast graphics package, produced end to end with AI in Google Flow.

Concept, direction and production by me, made with AI. Logo supplied by me.

The story

Seventy thousand people on hold

A packed stadium at night, and every fan and player in it is stuck on hold with the big guys: phones to ears, players standing still at midfield, the big screen reading PLEASE HOLD, the hold timer at 3:12:47. The quarterback says it flatly: “Still… on hold.”

Then a whistle. Frankie, in a referee shirt, throws the flag: “Flag on the play! Holding!” The quarterback’s phone lights up pink. “Whoa! Flamingo picked up!” The stadium erupts in pink, the announcer lands the offer in four words, “Flamingo picks up. First ring.”, and Frankie takes his place on top of the ball for the end card: “FlaminGO Mobile support.” Jingle: “Let’s go, flaminGO!”

The spot exists to show the campaign can play at national big-game scale: a photoreal stadium, an epic score and a broadcast graphics package, with the mascot still tiny and still on model in the middle of it.

The references

Four locks before a single shot

As with the first two spots, the references win. Shot prompts name what each attached image is and describe only the shot; they never re-describe Frankie in words.

R1 · Frankie, refereeBuilt with a single-change edit of the locked Frankie reference: add a tiny referee shirt with black and white stripes and a silver whistle on a black cord. Head, face, feathers, legs and size unchanged.
R2 · The stadiumA wide night plate of a fictional stadium: packed stands, floodlight towers, plain field markings, blank video screen. No team logos, no league logos, no numbers, no readable text anywhere, so every word on screen in the finished film is a graphic placed in the edit.
R3 · The quarterbackThe player from Spot 01, in a plain navy jersey and a silver helmet pushed back so his face is clear. Flow later refused the close shots as a prominent person, so his face was changed with an edit and every later player shot uses that face.
R4 · The scale frameFrankie at grass level beside the football: from his feet to the top of his head he is about half the length of the ball, like a small toy figurine. This frame is attached to every Frankie shot, and two fix prompts (one third smaller; match the reference exactly) repair any frame where he drifts big or off model.

Animation

Six shots, one continuous take each

Every shot was generated in Google Flow with Omni Flash 1.1 from an approved start frame, rendered at six seconds and trimmed in the edit. Motion prompts describe movement only, then the same locks: Frankie keeps his exact size, shirt and proportions; the player keeps his face; one continuous, unbroken shot, no cuts.

S1 · 0.0–2.3sSeventy thousand on hold. A slow aerial glide over the stands. Nobody moves much; a few fans shift and sigh under the tinny hold music.
S2 · 2.3–4.7sStill on hold. The quarterback, phone to his helmet, says his line to camera and sighs. The final take is a later retake whose own voice had the pause the line needed: “Still… on hold.”
S3 · 4.7–10.3sFlag on the play. Whistle blast, the yellow flag thrown high and tumbling in slow motion, wings out for the call. The last frame is held for just over half a second under the penalty card so HOLDING gets its screen time.
S4 · 10.3–12.7sFirst ring. The phone glows pink and the support card lands beside his face. An earlier version of this beat named Maya; it was replaced because a viewer meeting this spot alone does not know who Maya is.
S5 · 12.7–15.0sThe crowd erupts. Phones up and glowing pink, confetti cannons firing, a fast sweeping move across the stands under the announcer line and the matching kinetic super.
S6 · 15.0–23.5sThe ref rests his case. Frankie stands on the ball, wings folded, while the end card builds in the right two-thirds of the frame: logo, “No holding.”, “Let’s go, flaminGO!” and the fine print. The shot ends on a frame with his eyes open; the clip’s last frames start a blink, so the cut leaves before it.

Voices

The sync belongs to the performance

Frankie is voiced the same way as the first two spots: the Flow performance run through Speechify Voice Changer into Lindsey (F), accent on. One lesson is written into this cut: an early version time-stretched his line to fit the beak and distorted it, so the final film uses the unstretched read, a touch lower, with a little stadium air (light compression, low-mid warmth and a short reverb) to sit him in the space.

The quarterback’s two lines are the clips’ own voices, kept because their sync is exact, including the retaken “Still… on hold.” Pickup lines by text to speech use Speechify’s Walter, a calm, warm Canadian read. The announcer is Julien, the same voice as the earlier spots, with new lines for this one. The crowd boo after the whistle is the audio of a generated crowd clip, dipped under Frankie’s line instead of stopping dead, and fading out after HOLDING.

Music and sound

An epic score that ends on a jingle

The score is a new Suno production, written for this spot: an epic cinematic sports anthem at 104 BPM in C major, tense low strings and a ticking clock at the top, then a stadium drum hit into full orchestra, brass fanfare, marching snares, choir chants and stomp-clap, building to a clean final hit. It was joined in the edit so its final hit lands exactly on the existing sung jingle.

Sound design carries the joke: tinny hold music echoing around the stadium PA, cut dead by the whistle; a filtered rise under the flag toss into a boom on “Holding!”; one cheerful ring; confetti cannon booms and a full crowd roar from the S5 clip; applause under the end card. Dialogue gets a light room throughout so every voice sounds like it is in the same stadium.

Graphics and edit

A broadcast package, built like a broadcast

The graphics were designed as a real game package, set in Barlow Condensed for the sports elements and Fredoka and Figtree for the brand, and built as a separate overlay package with its own alpha track, stills and source files:

  • The PLEASE HOLD jumbotron, tracked onto the stadium screen and enlarged across versions until it read at a glance.
  • The yellow penalty card: FLAG ON THE PLAY, with HOLDING slamming in on the word, plus a short camera shake on the hit.
  • The FIRST RING support card beside the quarterback. It went through several fuller versions and was simplified twice, because a card that explains everything explains nothing at game speed.
  • Kinetic supers over the crowd (FLAMINGO PICKS UP. / FIRST RING.), the corner wordmark bug, and the end card build.
  • One colour rule across the whole spot: pink is reserved for flaminGO. Everything from the big guys, the hold tag, the jumbotron, the penalty slate, is grey and slate.

Picture finish is a filmic grade with a vignette and fine grain across all six shots, slow push-ins on the quarterback and the closing shot, and a mix at -16 LUFS.

Fifteen cuts, briefly

  • v1 assembled the six shots with the full overlay package; v2 fixed the stretched Frankie line.
  • v3 added the paused quarterback read, the enlarged jumbotron and the new announcer structure.
  • v4 and v5 brought in the retaken player shots, the new closing shot and the crowd boo.
  • v6 replaced the Maya beat and simplified the support card; v7 simplified it again.
  • v8 to v11 gave HOLDING its freeze frame, reserved pink for flaminGO, and named the big guys on the hold tag.
  • v12 to v14 removed the score bug, cleaned the tag and raised the closing announcer line.
  • v15, the final cut: a half-second of silent stare after “Still… on hold.”, the announcer super in two lines, and the boo riding under Frankie instead of cutting out.
