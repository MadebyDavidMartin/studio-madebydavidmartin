Production guide · spec commercial

The 2026 cut: same Juno, same street, made for now.

A 30-second social-first spot for young adults. It opens like a creator post, drops into a bouncy modern jingle, and lands on a hard-flash packshot. Juno and Maple Street come back, updated. Built to work in both vertical and widescreen.

30 s9:16 and 16:924 fpsGoogle Flow · Omni FlashSunoHyperFrames edit

The idea

"pov: the afternoon is mid." A bored Juno, a cold can, one cold wisp, and the street snaps into colour as her friends crowd into a 0.5x selfie. The rest plays like a fast photo dump of the best afternoon of the summer, ending on "open loud."

What makes it 2026

  • Opens with a creator-style hook and native-looking captions, so it reads as a post first and an ad second.
  • Phone-camera grammar: 0.5x ultra-wide selfie, handheld drift, hard direct flash, a photo-dump freeze sequence.
  • Short, low-key copy. Lowercase, deadpan, no forced slang. "no notes." is the only slang line.
  • Jingle in a bouncy jersey-club alt-pop style with chopped vocal hooks, built to loop.
  • Vertical first: text stays inside the safe zones of TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Rules carried over

  • Overlays never cover faces, the can, the glass or the action.
  • Nothing sexualised: no dancing, no bouncing bodies, no liquid spraying toward anyone, faces stay dry. Juno wears trousers.
  • Only text inside AI shots is the wordmark on the can. All other words are overlays.
  • Every motion prompt starts with "One continuous, unbroken shot, no cuts." and describes what you want, not what you do not want.
  • Music joins stay smooth and even in level; no added crack sound effect.

Look and brand

A refreshed identity for the same cherry cola: a lowercase wordmark with a cherry for the dot on the i, a slim can, and a hard-flash product look against acid lime.

Palette

Cherry #E5112B Milk #F6F1EA Cola #2A0F0A Acid lime #C9F23A

Type

  • Wordmark and headlines: Unbounded ExtraBold, lowercase.
  • Captions and stickers: Inter Tight Black.
  • Small labels: Space Mono.

Camera and colour

  • People shots: modern phone realism, slightly wide lens, natural skin texture, light grain, golden late-afternoon light.
  • Product shots: hard direct flash, crisp shadows, glossy cherry-red acrylic, acid-lime seamless backdrop.
  • Opening is washed out and hazy; colour floods in on the drop.
  • Wardrobe: relaxed, oversized streetwear in cream, denim and cherry red.

Shots

Nine generated shots plus one photo-dump moment built in the edit. For each shot, make the keyframe image, then animate it with frames to video using that image as the first frame. Make each shot twice: once in Landscape 16:9 and once in Portrait 9:16, using the framing note for each.

Caption style

pov: the afternoon is mid
  • Lowercase Inter Tight Black, white with a dark outline, like a native caption.
  • Appears word by word with the voice.

Shout-back stickers

real cherry.big fizz.ice cold.no notes.
  • Acid-lime rounded stickers with a hard offset shadow, tilted, popping in on each shout.
  • Placed beside the product, never over it.

Safe zones

  • 9:16: keep text out of the top 220 px, the bottom 420 px and the right 140 px of a 1080x1920 frame (app buttons and captions sit there).
  • 16:9: text in the left or right third, away from faces.

End card

  • Big red wordmark, "open loud." in Inter Tight Black, "cherry cola · 355 mL" in Space Mono, fine print "spec ad. fizzy pop is a fictional brand."
  • 9:16: stacked in the top half above the can. 16:9: left column, can on the right.
  • Light film grain over the whole spot; a single white flash frame only on the drop.
