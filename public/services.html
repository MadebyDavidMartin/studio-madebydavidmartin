Process

How a commission runs

A fixed process, agreed in writing before money moves. You always know what stage the film is at and what it will cost.

Step one

Brief and quote

You tell me what the film has to do. I reply with a straight answer on fit, timing and an exact price. No hourly billing, ever.

Step two

Concept stage

Script, visual look and hero stills. The pictures are locked before anything moves, so the film you approve is the film you get.

Step three

Production

The film is built shot by shot against the locked stills, with progress previews at the points we agreed.

Step four

Delivery

Finished files in every format you need, with two structured rounds of revisions included.

Questions

Before you write

How long does a film take?

Most single films deliver within two to three weeks of the concept lock. If you have a hard date, say so in your first message and I will tell you plainly whether it can be met.

What do you need from us?

A short brief: the product, the audience, where the film will run and the date you need it. Brand assets help; if they do not exist yet, the concept stage creates the look.

Is the work made with AI?

Yes, and directed by me. AI is the production method; the concept, the casting of performances, the edit decisions and the standard it is finished to are a director’s. That is why the work looks the way it does.

Who owns the finished film?

You do, for the uses we agree in the quote. Full terms are set out in writing before production begins.

Can you make a Japanese version?

Yes. Japanese versions are produced from the same locked production, not subtitled after the fact. SKYLET above is the example.
