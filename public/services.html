How a project runs

The brief

You tell me what the film has to do and when you need it. One call, or one email thread.

Script, look and hero stills, locked before anything moves. This stage is paid, and credited in full toward the film.

Step three

Production

The film is built shot by shot against the locked stills, so what you approved is what you get.

Delivery

Finished files in the formats you need, with cutdowns where we agreed them.
