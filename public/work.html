Proposals

Creative storytelling under real business constraints.

A proposal is an argument with a visual system. I direct proposal production the same way I direct anything else: one story, one standard, nothing ships unreviewed.

The system

Requirements → Knowledge → Strategy → Structure → Draft → Evidence → Review → Compliance → Approval.

The result

A complex RFP’s first draft moves from hours of manual work to roughly 30 minutes. Less time assembling information; more time strengthening the argument.
