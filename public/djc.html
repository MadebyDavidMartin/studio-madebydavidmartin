The property

Two decades building original media at serious scale.

DJC is character-based content, music, animation, video, storytelling and digital distribution, directed and managed at scale.

What it is.

An original media property with recurring characters, formats and a consistent visual identity. The catalogue spans animated stories, songs, educational content and character series, all produced to one standard and one canon.

How it grew.

Upload by upload, year by year. The channel compounded into a quarter billion lifetime views and 288K subscribers, with a 500+ video FAST-ready catalogue.

The story is not a viral moment. It is longevity: the ability to keep producing, keep the standard, and keep an audience coming back, year after year.

Case study

From 2D to 3D.

Rebuilding a flagship property for a new production era, without losing what made it work.

Bob and Kale started as 2D originals, drawn and animated by hand. The characters, the stories and the visual identity were established the slow way, which is why they lasted. Years later, the duo was rebuilt through an AI-assisted 3D pipeline with locked character references: the same characters, the same world, the same stories, now produced at a different scale and fidelity. The 2D originals proved the idea. The 3D rebuild proved the production system could carry that idea into a new era without changing its identity. Watch them side by side: the idea survived the transition intact.

Creative direction

I direct the content system.

My involvement runs through the whole pipeline, from the first idea to the published video and everything the catalogue becomes afterward.

Concept and world development

Original characters, recurring properties, story formats and the visual identity of the property. The world has an internal logic, and every new piece of content has to earn its place in it.

Story and script

Story development, format design and scripting. Stories are built for the audience: clear structure, repeatable formats, and pacing that holds young viewers without talking down to them.

Visual development

Character design, art direction and visual standards. A series bible keeps every production on model, so the catalogue reads as one property instead of a pile of unrelated videos.

Music and audio direction

Songs, scoring and sound design direction. Music is a first-class part of the property, not decoration added at the end.

Production and editing

Hands-on production and final edit on the catalogue. Every upload is reviewed before release: story, character, sound, packaging.

Programming and distribution

Content packaging, YouTube publishing and optimization, audience-focused programming, catalogue management. The work does not end at export; it ends when the audience finds it.

The production system

Repeatable workflows, serious volume.

460+ published videos do not happen by accident. They happen because the production system is designed for volume without losing the standard.

Designed for repetition

Formats, templates and production checklists turn each video from a bespoke project into a reliable run of a proven system. New ideas enter the system; finished videos come out.

Quality gates

Review points at story, visual, audio and packaging stages. The standard is enforced by process, not by memory, which is what lets the catalogue stay consistent at scale, across multiple production eras.

Catalogue thinking

Every video is produced as part of a library, not a one-off. Metadata, series structure and repackaging (including a 500+ video FAST-ready catalogue) keep old work earning new audiences.

Technology that serves the work

Production moved from hand-drawn frames to digital pipelines to AI-assisted workflows. The tools changed; the directing did not. Locked references, reviewed takes, one canon.

The AI evolution

Human-directed. AI-assisted.

DJC has become a practical laboratory for folding AI into real creative production, without giving up human direction or editorial control.

I use AI to extend what I can make, not to decide what gets made. I direct the creative intent, the structure, the pacing, the characters, the storytelling and the final editorial decisions. The technology executes; the direction stays human.

Where AI helps

Music generation and arrangement, visual development and reference generation, video production assistance, editing and repackaging workflows. Tools like Suno for music, ChatGPT for development and scripting support, Google Flow and AI-assisted visual and video generation for production, OpusClip for repackaging, and PowerDirector in the edit. Each tool has a defined job inside a directed process.

Where it never decides

What the story is. Whether a character stays on model. Whether a take ships. Whether the work is good enough. Those calls are the director’s, and the process is built so they always land with a human.

Why a catalogue is the right laboratory

A large catalogue is unforgiving: inconsistency shows immediately across hundreds of videos. That makes DJC an unusually honest test of whether AI-assisted workflows can hold a creative standard at scale. They can, when they are directed.

Evolution

One property, many production eras.

DJC has been in continuous production since 2006. The property survived every change in technology by adapting the workflow and protecting the creative standard.

  1. 2006 · The beginning

    Original productions built by hand. Characters, stories and music created from nothing, establishing the property’s voice and visual identity.

  2. The 2010s · Scale

    The catalogue grows into a serious YouTube property. Repeatable production systems take shape; the audience compounds into hundreds of millions of views.

  3. The 2020s · The AI era

    AI-assisted production enters the workflow: music, visuals, video and repackaging. Output expands, the standard holds, and the catalogue passes 500 FAST-ready videos.

  4. Today · A living catalogue

    460+ published videos, 288K subscribers, 20+ hours of content. Still directed, still produced, still evolving.
