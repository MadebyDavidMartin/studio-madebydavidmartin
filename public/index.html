Problems I solve.

The short version of this site: the problems that land on my desk, and what happens next.

AI spend is rising and nothing is changing.

I turn AI into governed, adopted capability that survives daily use: agents draft, people decide.

Knowledge lives in inboxes and leaves when people do.

I build governed knowledge bases teams can search and trust. One source, 86 files, used every day.

The company cannot explain itself on video, and AI video looks fake.

I direct film-led work end to end, including Occupied, a photorealistic AI thriller where the same face holds in every shot.

Leadership has no real view of the pipeline.

I consolidated 1,717 pursuits into one register with a lifecycle, a scorecard and a dashboard, so decisions run on data.

Content cannot keep pace with the market.

I rebuilt the workflow and took one social post from about four and a half hours to about forty minutes.

The brand looks different in every team's hands.

I build design and brand systems that hold across every output. I have maintained one inside a technology company for over a decade.

The launch cannot wait for the product to exist.

I direct concept films for products still on the drawing board, like the ORIVEX ARC-7 film.

The product is technical and buyers do not get it.

I make hard subjects clear on film, including a six-part cybersecurity series in one visual world.

Owned audience is the asset everyone wants and few can build.

I created and directed a children’s catalogue of 460+ videos with a quarter billion views.

Expansion into Japan needs more than translation.

I spent ten years working in Japan and direct in both languages. This site has a full Japanese edition.
